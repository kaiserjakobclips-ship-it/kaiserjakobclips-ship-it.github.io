2. What clippen is

clippen is a private, self-hosted tool used only by its operator to publish short video clips to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

3. Data processed

When an account is connected, clippen receives and stores only what is needed to publish videos:

  • TikTok (Login Kit / Content Posting API): access and refresh tokens, open ID, display name, avatar and profile link of the connected account; upload and publishing status of videos.
  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): access and refresh tokens, channel ID, channel name and thumbnail; upload and publishing status of videos.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions created by the operator.

clippen does not access followers, messages, comments, contacts or data of other users.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The data is used solely to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts and to show the publishing status (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

5. Storage and retention

All data is stored on the operator's own computer/server (self-hosted). Tokens and account data are kept until the account is disconnected and are then deleted. Video files are deleted when they are no longer needed.

6. Sharing

Data is not sold and not shared with third parties, except with the platform the video is published to (TikTok, Google/YouTube). To generate titles and captions, transcripts of the operator's own clips may be sent to OpenAI; no TikTok or YouTube account data is included.

7. YouTube API Services

clippen uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. TikTok

See the TikTok Privacy Policy (https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy). Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

9. Cookies and tracking

clippen uses no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This website itself is hosted by Google Sites; see the Google Privacy Policy.
